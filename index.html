What changes

A browser tab, minus the browser.

01

Real dock presence

Its own icon, its own app name, its own Cmd+Tab slot — not "Chrome" or "Safari" buried under forty tabs.

02

Camera & mic that work

Proper hardened-runtime entitlements, so macOS actually prompts you for access instead of hanging forever.

03

Native notifications

Messages and calls land in Notification Center like any other Mac app — no more missed Snaps because a tab was backgrounded.

04

Stays logged in

Session persists across restarts. Open it, you're in — same as the last time you closed it.

05

No marketing clutter

"Download the app," Ads Manager popups, and other web-only nags are stripped out client-side.

06

It's the real thing

Every Snap, chat, and login goes straight to Snapchat's own servers. Nothing is proxied, logged, or altered.
